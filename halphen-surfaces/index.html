Catalogue

Each entry records the coefficient field, smooth cubic, ordered cluster of nine centres, infinitely-near data when present, normal and extension data, and the classes of the prime \((-2)\)-curves in the ordered basis \((L,E_1,\ldots,E_9)\).

The class matrix is presented uniformly: one row per prime \((-2)\)-curve and exactly ten columns, corresponding to \(L,E_1,\ldots,E_9\).
