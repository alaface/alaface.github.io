Catalogue

The catalogue records the twenty-six explicit plane models in the supplied LaTeX source, at the natural index \(m=\exp(E_8/\Lambda)>1\). It organizes the ADE root type, extension orbit, middle group and number of line components. The underlying plane-model data use the basis \((L,E_1,\ldots,E_9)\).

The split \(D_6+2A_1\) extension is absent because its middle group is \((\mathbb Z/2)^3\), which cannot be a subgroup of a smooth elliptic curve; the two nonzero extension orbits are represented.
