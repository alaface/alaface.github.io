Uniform catalogue convention

Every surface is presented in the same order: field and cubic, cluster, infinitely-near data, normal, extension, and verification, and one final class matrix of the (-2)-curves.

The matrix always has ten columns, corresponding to the ordered basis \((L,E_1,\ldots,E_9)\). Its number of rows is the number of prime \((-2)\)-components in the affine ADE configuration and therefore legitimately varies from one root type to another.
